WHO WE ARE

BUILDING TRUST
THROUGH STEEL & STONE

Fabtech Infra Buildcon is a registered proprietorship firm owned and operated by Tanveer Akhter, specializing in the execution of all types of government tenders across civil engineering, road development, water infrastructure, and public works.

With a proven track record of delivering quality projects on time, we have established strong professional relationships with government bodies and elected representatives across the region.

GOVERNMENT REGISTERED FIRM
Fully licensed and registered to bid for and execute all categories of government tenders.
PROVEN EXECUTION RECORD
Successful completion of multiple civil and infrastructure tenders across diverse project types.
ON-TIME DELIVERY
Committed to meeting project timelines and quality benchmarks for all government contracts.
WHAT WE DO

OUR CORE SERVICES

End-to-end government tender execution across all civil infrastructure domains.

01
CIVIL CONSTRUCTION
Government buildings, schools, hospitals, community halls and public infrastructure development.
02
ROAD DEVELOPMENT
Construction and repair of roads, bridges, culverts under PMGSY and state PWD tenders.
03
WATER SUPPLY WORKS
Piped water supply, overhead tanks, tube wells, and Jal Jeevan Mission project execution.
04
ELECTRICAL PROJECTS
Street lighting, rural electrification, sub-station works and solar installations under government tenders.
05
DRAINAGE & SEWERAGE
Stormwater drainage, sewage treatment, pipeline work and AMRUT scheme projects.
06
ALL-TYPE TENDERS
Bidding, documentation, and execution of all categories — district, state, and central government contracts.
